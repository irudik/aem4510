AEM 4510 Permit Market Game

Each team runs a firm with its own abatement costs. Permits are sold in a uniform-price auction, then retraded in a live secondary market with an order book. Round 2 tightens the cap. Teams join the Student Portal with a team name; the instructor runs auctions, timers, and the market from the Admin Dashboard.
