## Course Information

**Course sections:** AEM 4510, AEM 5510, ECON 3865

**Professor:** Ivan Rudik (ir229)

This page provides the general course syllabus. See [Canvas](https://canvas.cornell.edu/) for the current course schedule, teaching team, office hours, assignment deadlines, exam logistics, and announcements.

**Prerequisites:** MATH 1110, and AEM 2600 or ECON 3030.

**Course description:** This class focuses on the role of the environment in the theory and practice of economics. It uses microeconomic analysis at the intermediate level and incorporates real-world examples. It examines market failures, externalities, benefit-cost analysis, non-market valuation techniques, cost-effective policy instruments, financial instruments, and recent applied work on the economics of the environment.

**Course requirements:** Students are expected to prepare for class, do the readings, and complete all problem sets and prelims.

## Readings and Lecture Slides

- *Markets and the Environment* by Keohane and Olmstead is required.
- Other readings are papers linked on the [course topics and lecture slides page](/lecture-notes/), or freely available through the [Cornell library](https://www.library.cornell.edu/) or [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/).
- [*Causal Inference: The Mixtape*](https://mixtape.scunning.com/) by Cunningham is free and useful for background on the econometric techniques used in readings in the second half of the course, or for students interested in environmental economics research.

## Problem Sets

There are four problem sets, one before each prelim. You may consult with me or the teaching assistants if you have questions or need guidance. See Canvas for deadlines.

- You have unlimited submissions before the deadline. No late problem sets will be accepted because the answer key is posted immediately after the deadline. Aim to submit early enough to handle any problems.
- You may work in teams of up to three. Submit one problem set per team through Gradescope on Canvas, with all group members' names on it.
- If you work on paper, scan and convert your work to a PDF using a tool such as [Adobe Scan](https://www.adobe.com/acrobat/mobile/scanner-app.html). Pictures will not be accepted and will receive a zero.
- You are responsible for submitting the correct file. Unreadable or corrupted files will not be accepted. Your final submission before the deadline is what will be graded.

## Learning Outcomes

As a result of participating in class, you will be able to:

- Discuss the design and effect of environmental policy in theory and real-world practice. AEM 5510 also covers the consequences of environmental policy in second-best environments.
- Use survey and revealed preference methods to value the environment.
- Explain how financial instruments provide information about the environment. AEM 5510 also covers how green financial instruments work.
- Explain how economists measure the economic costs of environmental change.
